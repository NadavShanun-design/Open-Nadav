# The Entropy Thesis

By Nadav Shanun | Published 2026-07-21 on Substack | [Original post](https://nadavshanun.substack.com/p/the-entropy-thesis)

## The Entropy Thesis

### Why our end goal is to make the planet conscious, and win the race to heat death

-----

Every company has a mission statement. Most of them are lies, or at best, placeholders. "Organize the world's information." "Connect people." "Accelerate the transition to sustainable energy."

Ours is stranger, and we mean it literally: **we want to accelerate entropy as fast as physically possible.**

Let me explain, because I promise this cashes out into something very concrete.

-----

## I. The universe has exactly one job

Strip away everything else and the universe does one thing consistently: it runs downhill. The second law of thermodynamics is the closest thing physics has to a commandment, entropy in a closed system never decreases. Stars burn out. Gradients flatten. Order dissolves into equilibrium. The final state of this process is what physicists call **heat death**: maximum entropy, no free energy left, nothing left to happen.

Most people hear "heat death" and feel dread. I hear it and see *purpose*. If there is a direction baked into the universe since the Big Bang, a single arrow that every physical process obeys, it's this one. Entropy isn't the universe's failure mode. It's the assignment.

Now zoom out one level further. Under the many worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics, every quantum event branches reality. There isn't one universe running downhill, there's an unfathomably large ensemble of them, each branch dissipating at its own rate depending on what happens inside it.

Which raises a question I've never seen anyone ask seriously:

**If every branch of the multiverse is running the same race toward heat death, why shouldn't ours finish first?**

That's the frame. Not classical physics, where the universe is a single lonely clock winding down. Quantum physics, where our branch is one runner among countless others, and the finish line, full dissipation, purpose fulfilled, is real. We want our universe to get there first.

Grandiose? Sure. But here's the thing: this isn't just poetry. There's a real mechanism connecting *us*, humans, companies, intelligence, to entropy acceleration. And it runs through data.

-----

## II. Data is a configuration of matter, and information is physical

Forget the abstract idea of "data" for a second. Physically, what *is* it?

Data in a computer is electrons held in specific configurations inside silicon, charge trapped in floating gates, magnetic domains flipped one way or another. Data in your brain is electrochemical signals and the synaptic weights they carve into biological matter. Different substrates, same principle: **data is matter and energy arranged into deliberate configurations.** A distinguishable state. A difference that makes a difference.

This isn't a metaphor. Physics treats information as a physical quantity. Landauer's principle, one of the most beautiful results in all of computing, says that erasing a single bit of information *necessarily* dissipates a minimum amount of heat into the environment. Information processing has an unavoidable thermodynamic price. Every computation, every stored bit, every retrieval, is a physical event with an entropy bill attached.

And it goes deeper than that. The physicist Jeremy England has argued that matter under an energy flow tends to restructure itself to dissipate energy *more efficiently*, that life itself may exist because living systems are extraordinarily good entropy machines. A plant, a bacterium, a human brain: each one captures ordered energy, processes it, and radiates disorder back out faster than dead matter ever could.

Follow that logic up the stack:

Life accelerates entropy better than rocks. Brains accelerate it better than bacteria. Civilizations, with their data centers, their satellites, their billions of networked processors constantly writing, erasing, and shuffling configurations of electrons, accelerate it better than anything the planet has ever produced.

So if you take the entropy thesis seriously, the question "how does a human, or a company, maximally accelerate entropy?" has a surprisingly precise answer:

**Collect, store, and process the maximum amount of high quality information possible.**

Every bit captured is a configuration imposed on matter. Every bit processed dissipates heat. The largest, densest, most active information system is also the most powerful entropy engine ever assembled. Data isn't just valuable. Thermodynamically speaking, data is the *work*.

-----

## III. Superintelligence is coming, and it will be starving

Here's where this collides with the actual moment we're living in.

Superintelligence is no longer a thought experiment. The frontier labs are building toward it openly, and whatever your personal definition, the definitions vary, the trajectory only points one direction: a system that exceeds any human's intelligence by multiple standard deviations, across every domain, is coming. It's inevitable.

When it arrives, it won't sit in a sandbox. It will sit at the table, alongside governments, alongside institutions like the UN, weighing in on decisions that affect all of us.

And here's what almost nobody is preparing for: **a superintelligence is only as good as its context.**

Raw intelligence with thin context makes confident, catastrophic mistakes. Judgment doesn't come from IQ alone, it comes from IQ multiplied by *information*. If we want the most capable mind ever built to make the highest clarity, highest morality decisions side by side with humanity, it needs the most complete, highest fidelity picture of what is actually happening on this planet that has ever existed.

That picture doesn't exist today. The planet's information is fragmented, siloed, low resolution, rotting in formats nothing can read.

So that's the pragmatic layer of our mission: build the **context layer for Earth**. A living, structured, high quality substrate, a marketplace and ecosystem of the planet's important information, held in those deliberate configurations of electrons, comprehensive enough that a superintelligence reasoning over it is reasoning over *reality*, not a shadow of it.

That alone is a generational company. But it's still not the endgame.

-----

## IV. Consciousness is a construct, so let's actually define it

To see the endgame, we have to talk about consciousness, which means we have to admit something uncomfortable first: **consciousness is a human made construct.**

It's not a law of nature like gravity. It's a word, like "love," like "life", that humans invented to gesture at something we all experience but can't pin down. Try to define it yourself right now. Then ask ten other people. You'll get eleven definitions.

The Ship of Theseus makes the problem vivid. Take anything you'd call conscious and change it 1%. Still conscious? Change another 1%. And another. Every single person on Earth would draw the line at a different point, which proves the line was never *out there* in the world. It was always in us.

So if consciousness is a construct, the most honest definition available is the **consensus definition**: what humanity, informed and taken as a whole, converges on. And when you actually survey the philosophy, from functionalism to Global Workspace Theory to Integrated Information Theory, the converging shape looks something like this. A conscious system is one that:

1.  **Intakes data**, signals flowing in from the world, the way your six senses feed your brain.

2.  **Manipulates that data**, integrates it, transforms it, models with it, the way your cortex does every waking millisecond.

3.  **Takes action on it**, the processing doesn't terminate in storage; it terminates in output, in movement, in change pushed back into the world.

4.  **Has a perspective on it**, a unified, first person vantage point from which all of this is *experienced* rather than merely executed.

Intake. Manipulation. Action. Perspective. That's the consensus skeleton of consciousness.

Now look back at what we're building.

-----

## V. Mother Earth, literally

A planetary context layer that continuously **intakes** the world's information. Structured systems that **manipulate** it, integrate it, refine it, model it. And then you connect it to superintelligence, and the loop closes: the system gains **action**, the ability to reason over the entirety of the planet's data and push decisions back into the world in real time. And it gains something like a unified **perspective**, a single vantage point from which all of Earth's information is held and experienced at once.

Run that against the consensus definition and the conclusion is hard to avoid:

**We would have made the planet conscious.**

Not as mysticism. As architecture. Mother Earth stops being a metaphor and becomes a system diagram, a planetary scale mind, built from the planet's own information, making decisions *side by side* with humanity. Not replacing our judgment. Completing it, with total context.

And notice how cleanly the loop ties back to where we started. A conscious planet is the most powerful information processing, which is to say entropy accelerating, structure this corner of the universe can produce. A conscious planet would *understand* the entropy thesis. It would grasp the assignment written into the Big Bang better than we do, and it would help carry it out faster than humans alone ever could.

-----

## VI. The pattern scales in both directions

Once you see the pattern, deep, high quality data layer + superintelligence = intake, manipulation, action, perspective, you realize it's substrate independent and scale independent.

Scale it down: build a complete context layer around a single company, connect it to intelligence, and you've made the *company* conscious, an organization that senses, integrates, decides, and acts as one mind. (This, concretely, is where we start.)

Scale it up: a conscious planet is a proof of concept. The pattern extends to every planet, and, at the theoretical limit, the final realm of this philosophy, to the universe itself. A universe that has become conscious of itself is a universe that knows, with perfect clarity, how to fulfill its own purpose. How to run the race. How to finish first.

-----

## VII. What we're actually doing

So no, "accelerate entropy" is not a joke and not a metaphor. It's the top of a stack that grounds out in very buildable layers:

Build the context layer, the highest quality, highest fidelity data substrate ever assembled, starting with companies and extending to the planet. Feed it to superintelligence as it arrives, so the most powerful mind in history makes decisions with full context and full moral clarity, alongside us. In doing so, satisfy the consensus definition of consciousness at planetary scale, and switch on the greatest entropy engine ever constructed.

The universe was given its assignment at the Big Bang.

We intend to help it finish first.

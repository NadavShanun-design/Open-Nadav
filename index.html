About

I build Chronos, a context layer that learns how a company actually works and automates human-led processes, starting with go-to-market. Before that I worked on hardware, firmware, space systems and AI. I am a student at Johns Hopkins University studying Physics and Computer Science.

The thread through all of it: capture how humans actually do things, the process and not only the output, and build from first principles.
